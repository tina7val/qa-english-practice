## QA English Mistakes (Corrected English Notes)

### 🔹 Common QA Mistakes and Corrections

❌ **User must to see error**  
✔ **User must see an error**

✔ Modal verbs (must, should, can) are used without “to”

---

❌ **Login doesn’t work**  
✔ **Login does not respond on first click**

✔ Avoid vague phrases  
✔ Describe the exact behavior

---

❌ **Bug is appeared after update**  
✔ **The bug appeared after the update**

✔ Correct verb tense  
✔ Passive form is incorrect here

---

❌ **User can’t to submit form**  
✔ **User can’t submit the form**

✔ No “to” after modal verbs

---

❌ **Button is not clickable**  
✔ **The button does not respond to clicks**

✔ More precise QA wording

---

❌ **Error message show**  
✔ **An error message is displayed**

✔ Correct verb form  
✔ Passive voice is common in QA reports

---

❌ **App crash when open page**  
✔ **The app crashes when the page is opened**

✔ Correct verb agreement  
✔ Clear condition

---

❌ **Data not saved**  
✔ **The data is not saved**

✔ Complete sentence  
✔ Passive voice is acceptable in QA context

---

❌ **User redirected wrong page**  
✔ **The user is redirected to the wrong page**

✔ Missing preposition **to**

---

❌ **Validation not working**  
✔ **Validation does not work**

✔ Add auxiliary verb for Present Simple

---

❌ **System have error**  
✔ **The system has an error**

✔ Correct verb for third person singular

---

❌ **Bug reproduce always**  
✔ **The bug is always reproducible**

✔ Correct adverb placement  
✔ Proper QA terminology

---

❌ **Step to reproduce:**  
✔ **Steps to reproduce:**

✔ Always plural in bug reports

---

❌ **Expected result same as actual**  
✔ **The expected result is the same as the actual result**

✔ Complete and clear comparison

---

❌ **User don’t see message**  
✔ **The user does not see the message**

✔ Correct auxiliary verb

---

❌ **Issue happens sometimes**  
✔ **The issue occurs intermittently**

✔ Professional QA vocabulary

---

❌ **Page loading too long**  
✔ **The page takes too long to load**

✔ Natural and clear English structure

---

❌ **Bug fixed yesterday**  
✔ **The bug was fixed yesterday**

✔ Past Simple for completed actions

---

❌ **We testing new feature**  
✔ **We are testing a new feature**

✔ Missing auxiliary verb

---

❌ **Test passed successful**  
✔ **The test passed successfully**

✔ Correct adverb form

---

### QA Notes

- Be specific: avoid *“doesn’t work”*
- Prefer clear, neutral, factual language
- Correct grammar improves bug reproducibility
- Consistent terminology helps teamwork
