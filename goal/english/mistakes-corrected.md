## Corrected English Notes

### 🔹 Common QA Mistakes and Corrections

❌ **User must to see error**  
✔ **User must see an error**

✔ Correct use of the modal verb **must**  
✖ “to” is not used after modal verbs

---

❌ **Login doesn’t work**  
✔ **Login does not respond on first click**

✔ More specific and professional QA wording  
✔ Clearly describes the actual issue instead of a generic statement

---

### 💡 QA Note

- Avoid vague phrases like *“doesn’t work”*
- Always describe **what exactly happens** and **under what conditions**
- Clear language helps developers reproduce and fix issues faster
